Privacy Policy — ERA Systems

Last updated: August 2026

ERA Systems ("we", "us") builds and operates a business operations platform, including WhatsApp-based communication assistants ("the Service"), used by independent client businesses to handle both their customer-facing communication and their own internal staff/operations communication.

Who this applies to

This policy covers data handled by ERA Systems while operating the Service on behalf of a client business. Each client business owns its own WhatsApp Business number and its relationships with its own customers and staff; ERA Systems acts as their technology provider.

What we collect

  • Messages sent to and from a client business's WhatsApp number, and associated metadata (timestamps, message status, phone numbers) -- covering both customer conversations and internal staff/operations messages on that same number.
  • Information a customer provides in conversation with a client business (e.g. name, order/booking details) needed to fulfill that business's own service to the customer.
  • Information staff exchange while coordinating that business's own internal operations (e.g. task handoffs, internal notifications).
  • Basic account and configuration information for the client business itself (business name, staff contact details, service preferences).

How we use it

  • To operate the messaging/automation service for the client business the data belongs to, across both its customer-facing and internal operations communication.
  • To route, store, and display conversations so the client business (and its staff) can respond to its own customers and coordinate internally.
  • To maintain and improve the reliability of the Service.

We do not sell customer or staff data, and we do not use one client business's data for another business's benefit.

Sharing

Data is shared only: with the client business the conversation belongs to; with WhatsApp/Meta as required to deliver messages through the WhatsApp Business Platform; and with infrastructure providers (hosting, database) strictly to operate the Service.

Data retention

Conversation data is retained for as long as the client business's account is active, or as needed to provide the Service, unless a client business requests earlier deletion of its own data.

Security

Access to conversation data is restricted to the relevant client business and the ERA Systems personnel operating the Service. Data is stored on access-controlled infrastructure.

Your rights

If you are a customer or staff member communicating with a business that uses this Service and want your data corrected or deleted, contact that business directly, or reach us at hello@erasystemsltd.com and we will forward your request.

Children

The Service is intended for use by businesses and their staff communicating with their own adult customers, and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Service changes. Updates will be posted at this same URL.
